Liar's Luck

Privacy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy explains what the Liar's Luck two-player sound game processes when you play online.

Information used to run a match

When you create or join a room, the server temporarily processes your chosen player name, your room code, your answers, and your current match score. Your answer is kept private from the other player until both players have answered. These details are held in server memory to run the match; the game does not provide accounts and does not save match history or scores to a database.

Retention and connections

Room and match information is removed when the room is empty, the server restarts, or an inactive room expires (after up to three hours). To connect players and deliver the game, the hosting provider processes network connection data under its own terms and retention policies. This game does not include analytics, advertising, or in-game chat.

The installable app caches the game interface and app icons on your device for loading. It does not cache your room answers or match scores.

Children and player names

Use a nickname that does not identify you. Do not enter your real name, contact details, password, or other sensitive information. Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of online rooms. The service has no accounts or age-verification system.

Your choices and questions

You can leave a match to end your participation. For questions about this policy, contact the app developer through the contact details on the official app store listing.

Independent game

Liar's Luck is an independent game and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Roblox Corporation.
